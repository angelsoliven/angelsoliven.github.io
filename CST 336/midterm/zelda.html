Instructions

This browser generates images based on the filters you select. The reset button will clear any filters you selected. The entry dropdown list allows you to select images based on their category in the compendium of Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom. The entry name text box allows you to search for a specific entry in the same compendium. The reset button unchecks the filters you selected.

The images and names of the entities are pulled from Hyrule Compendium API. Page created by Angel Soliven: angelmsoliven@gmail.com
