Instructions

This browser generates Pokemon sprites based on the filters you select. The reset button will clear any filters you selected. Type refers to Pokemon type, the text box allows for filtering based on color, and generation refers to the first appearance of a Pokemon. There are only 10 valid colors: red, black, white, purple, blue, yellow, brown, pink, green, and gray. The images will be generated below the filter buttons.

The sprites and names of the Pokemon are pulled from PokeAPI. Page created by Angel Soliven: angelmsoliven@gmail.com
